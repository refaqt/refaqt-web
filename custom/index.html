How we work with you

Start small. Grow when it works.

Three ways to work together, from light to heavy. You can start with the first one and grow.

Level 1

Reference design with your part

We adapt a stage design to your motor, guide or screw. We test it and publish the files, the parts list and the test results. You give it to your customers as a starting point.

Level 2

A stage range for your products

We develop stages together with you, for your product range and your customers. You can sell them, or let workshops build them.

Level 3

Built and supported by us

Your customers who do not want to build the stage buy it ready from us. We add calibration, support and versions for special needs.

Ask for a stage

Commercial licence

The designs are open under the CERN-OHL-S licence. In short: a machine with the stage built in must have an open design too. A commercial licence removes that duty. Your customer can use and change the stage design, and keep their own machine design closed.

Ask about a licence →

Open by default

A design your customers can check.

Open files and open test results build trust. Every customer can see how the stage is made and how well it works.

  • The stage design, the parts list, the software and the test results stay open, also when a partner pays for the work.
  • Your part stays your product. Its inside design does not have to become open.
  • You can put your own name on the stage you sell. The names REFAQT and AQTUATOR need our agreement.
  • Any workshop can build the stage from the open files. So your customers are never stuck with one supplier.

How a project goes

From your parts to a stage on the market.

  1. 1

    We talk

    Which parts do you sell? What do your customers ask for? Which stage would help them most?

  2. 2

    We choose

    Together we choose a base design and the parts from your range that go in it.

  3. 3

    We adapt and test

    We change the design, build a stage and measure it. You get the files and the test results.

  4. 4

    You offer it

    We publish the design. You offer the stage to your customers. They build it, buy it from us, or take a licence.
