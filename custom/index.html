Why partners work with us

Sell the system, not only the part.

The need

Your customers build machines. They want an integrated motion system that works on day one, not a list of parts.

The problem

A proven stage design takes a lot of engineering: design, prototypes, measurements and documents. Few companies can do this for every customer.

Our solution

We adapt our proven stage designs to your parts, so the risk and the engineering cost stay low. You offer your customers a tested stage design, and every stage that is built uses your parts.

How the licences work

Open for everyone. Closed when your customer needs it.

Open licence

CERN-OHL-S

Anyone may build and use the design. Who sells a machine with the design inside, changed or not, must publish the design of the whole machine. Bought-in standard parts can stay closed.

Commercial licence

Through the licence codes

For companies that want to keep their machine design closed. With a licence code, your customer does not have to publish anything.

Ownership

Stays with REFAQT

We keep ownership of the design and build our next designs on it, so the design keeps getting better. You get licence rights.

  • New contacts for you: companies that download the open design and agree to share their details with you.
  • You can put your own name on the stage you sell. The names REFAQT and AQTUATOR need our agreement.
