# 2026-11-12 — A real REFAQT home page

**Role(s):** marketing, software

## What happened

- The home page used to show one headline and one product card. It now tells the full story, in
  this order: what REFAQT makes and why, the first product (AQTUATOR CS), why REFAQT exists (the
  problem, what is needed, the mission), how REFAQT works (open designs, marketplace and
  partners, commercial licences, custom engineering), the plan in five steps, an invitation to
  join the cooperative, and a contact form.
- The text comes from a summary of the founder's journey, vision and values. The founder
  approved that summary. It is in `refaqt/refaqt-business`, in
  `docs/strategy/2026-11-12_founder-journey-and-vision.md`.
- The order differs a little from the founder's first outline: "Why we exist" now comes before
  "How we work". A visitor first needs the problem and the mission. Then the business model
  makes sense, and the plan leads into "Join us".
- The plan follows the strategy diagram on the Notion vision page, without the step "Buy small
  CNC milling machine".
- The hero image shows four machines built from the real AQTUATOR CS model: a 5-axis milling
  machine, a laser cutter, a lathe and a gantry measuring machine (CMM). Frames, bases, spindles
  and heads are simple shapes. The founder asked for the layouts of three known machines: the
  Rownd desktop lathe (headstock on the left, a slanted wall behind the tool), the xTool
  MetalFab laser cutter (slat table, a gantry beam with the head on its front) and a classic
  gantry CMM. Only the layout was taken, not the look or the brand.
- The AQTUATOR CS page has a short "Join us" band at the end. It links to the home page.
- The form backend now accepts the home page form. Home page messages go to their own file in
  `refaqt/refaqt-business`.

## Decisions

- The picture has no labels. Only the description for screen readers names the machines, and
  the footer says that the machines are concepts in development.
- One form backend serves both pages, with one file per form.

## Open Questions

  sketch layouts and hand drawings, with no 3D render. Where is that render?
- The form backend is not deployed yet. Until it is, both forms say "not connected yet".
