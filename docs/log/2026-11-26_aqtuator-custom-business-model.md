# 2026-11-26 — AQTUATOR Custom page follows the new business model

**Role(s):** marketing, sales, business-dev

## What happened

- The AQTUATOR Custom page now tells the same story as our offer to Wittenstein (in the
  maqers repository: the offer "A precision stage design built around Wittenstein components").
- New headline: "Your parts, as a complete stage." The top text now speaks of an integrated
  motion system, and of any drive, motor, guide or feedback system.
- The three "Why" boxes are now need, problem and solution. The solution says that we adapt our
  proven stage designs, so the risk and the engineering cost stay low.
- "What we change" is now "Customizations".
- "How we work" now has the three parts of the offer: engineering (design, prototype and tests,
  fixed scope and price), licence codes for the partner's customers, and an optional licence for
  the partner. Under it: every design is built and tested, and we give second-line support.
- A new section explains the licences: open licence, commercial licence through the codes, and
  ownership that stays with REFAQT. It also names the new contacts the partner gets.
- "How a project goes" now follows the next steps of the offer: scope, quote, engineering
  agreement with design and tests, then the partner offers the design. The pilot sentence is gone.
- The contact heading is now "What do you want to build?". The words "makers" and "stage team"
  are gone. The home page card has the same message.
