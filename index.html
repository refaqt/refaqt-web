Why we exist

Manufacturing is built for mass production. We build for local.

The problem

Today's factories are built for mass production. Big plants need big series, big stock and long transport. The result is waste, CO₂ emissions, and products that nobody can repair.

What is needed

Small, modular machines that make high-quality parts locally and on demand. Machines that anyone can buy, build, repair and improve.

Our mission

We build small modules and machines that make local, on-demand manufacturing of mechanical parts possible.

How we work

Open designs. A network that builds them.

01

Open designs

We publish every module and machine under the CERN-OHL-S licence. Anyone may copy, build and change the design. If you share a changed design, you share it under the same licence, together with the designs it comes from.

02

Marketplace and partners

We sell machines, modules and parts. Partner members build them and give support to customers. Partners get bulk prices on parts, design and product tracking, build instructions that fit their own workshop, and technical support.

03

Commercial licences

Do you want to build our design into a closed product? A commercial licence removes the duty to share, so your own design can stay private.

04

Custom engineering

We help you adapt a module, or design a machine for your process.
